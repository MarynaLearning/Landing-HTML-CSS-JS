03 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

May, June, and September offer warm seas, sunny days, and thinner crowds than peak July–August. The shoulder season is ideal for sightseeing and island hopping.

Croatia is part of the EU and the Schengen Area. Many travelers can enter visa-free for up to 90 days, but check your country's specific requirements before booking.

Croatia adopted the euro (€) in 2023. Cards are widely accepted, though carrying some cash is handy for small islands and local markets.

Regular car and passenger ferries connect the mainland to major islands, with faster catamarans in summer. For flexibility, many visitors combine a rental car with ferry crossings.
